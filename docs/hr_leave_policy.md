# Leave policy (sample document, fictional company)

Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks before the first day of absence. A manager approves or declines the request within three working days.

Unused annual leave can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Days above this limit expire on 31 March.

Sick leave does not reduce annual leave. Employees must inform their manager on the first day of illness. A medical certificate is required when the absence lasts more than three consecutive days.

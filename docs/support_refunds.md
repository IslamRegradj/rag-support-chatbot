# Refund policy (sample document, fictional company)

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of the purchase date. After 30 days, refunds are not available, but the customer can cancel the subscription to avoid the next payment.

To request a refund, the customer opens a ticket from the Billing page and gives the order number. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Gift cards and one-time training sessions are not refundable once they have been used.

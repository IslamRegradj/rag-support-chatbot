# Account access (sample document, fictional company)

To reset a password, click the Forgot password link on the login page and enter the account email. The reset link arrives within a few minutes and stays valid for 24 hours. If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder before opening a support ticket.

After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes. The customer can wait for the lock to end or contact support to unlock it sooner.

Two-factor authentication can be turned on from the Security page of the account settings. Support can disable it only after verifying the customer's identity.
